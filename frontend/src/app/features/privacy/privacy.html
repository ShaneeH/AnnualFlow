Privacy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Annualflow is a small open source project. This page explains, in plain terms, what it keeps and why. You can browse the example dataset without signing in.

What is stored

  • When you sign in with Google: your name, email address, Google account ID and profile picture URL, and the date you joined. Nothing else from your Google account.
  • The reports you upload: the company, report name, type, date and currency, the figures extracted for each period, a fingerprint (SHA-256) of the PDF used to spot duplicates, and any insights you generate.
  • The PDF itself is not kept in the database. While a background upload is waiting to be processed it is held temporarily and cleared as soon as processing finishes.

Your reports are private to your account. Other users cannot see them.

OpenAI

To read a report, the PDF is uploaded to OpenAI's API and analysed there. If the upload fails or is not a financial report, that copy is deleted straight away. If it succeeds, the copy stays in OpenAI's file storage until you delete the report, or until your account is permanently deleted, and is then deleted too. When you ask for insights, only the stored figures for that period are sent, not the PDF. Requests are made with response storage turned off.

Cookies and local storage

There is one cookie: rh_session, an httpOnly sign-in cookie that lasts 7 days and is removed when you sign out. Your dashboard selections (company, period, comparison period, chart view and whether the insights panel is open) are saved in your browser's local storage under annualflow.dashboard.v1, and never leave your browser.

There is no analytics or tracking. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the sign-in button is loaded from Google when you open the sign-in page.

Your data

On your profile page you can export all of your data as a JSON file, and delete your account. Deleting hides everything straight away. If you sign in again within 30 days you can recover it. After that, your account and everything you uploaded are permanently deleted.
